⚙️ Engineering Trade-offs

Every engineering decision comes with advantages and disadvantages.

Decision| Advantages| Disadvantages
Simplicity vs Complexity| Easier maintenance| May limit advanced functionality
Performance vs Readability| Faster execution| Potentially harder to understand
Memory vs Speed| Faster access| Higher memory consumption
Build vs Buy| Greater control| Higher development cost
Security vs Convenience| Better protection| More friction
Flexibility vs Predictability| Adaptability| Harder to manage

The best engineering decision is rarely universally best. It depends on the requirements, constraints, and context.

🧭 Engineering Principles

1. Understand the problem before designing the solution.
2. Never assume a technology is the best simply because it is popular.
3. Simplicity is a strength, not a limitation.
4. Every optimization should have a reason.
5. Understand trade-offs before making decisions.
6. Learn from failure instead of hiding it.
7. Consider the people who will actually use the software.
8. Build systems that solve real problems.
