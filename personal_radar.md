«Exploring technologies, evaluating tools, tracking curiosity, and navigating my developer journey.»

🌌 What Is This?

The Tech Radar is my personal map of technologies, tools, frameworks, concepts, and ideas that I encounter throughout my software development journey.

It helps me distinguish between technologies I am actively learning, technologies I want to explore, tools I have experimented with, and technologies I understand well enough to use confidently.

The goal isn't to learn everything.

The goal is to learn the right things, understand them properly, and make informed technical decisions.

---

🧭 Radar Zones

🟢 ADOPT

Technologies and concepts I understand reasonably well and am comfortable using in appropriate projects.

🔵 EXPLORE

Technologies I am actively learning, experimenting with, or investigating.

🟡 WATCH

Technologies that interest me but aren't currently a priority.

🔴 HOLD

Technologies or approaches I have evaluated and decided not to prioritize right now.

HOLD doesn't necessarily mean a technology is bad. It may simply not fit my current goals.

🧠 My Technology Evaluation Criteria

Before investing significant time into a technology, I try to evaluate:

- Purpose: What problem does it solve?
- Fundamentals: What concepts does it depend on?
- Practicality: Can I use it to build something?
- Ecosystem: Does it have useful tools and community support?
- Relevance: Does it align with my goals?
- Trade-offs: What are its limitations?
- Learning cost: Is it worth learning right now?
